## 英文页面文案（主语言建议英文，覆盖面大）

**Tagline**: Neon Hearts: Lin Xia's Phone — an interactive romance told entirely through a phone.

**Short description**:

> You've just moved to Neon City. Three men are already texting you — a gentle-but-scheming
> gallery director, a childhood friend turned photographer, and a bartender who writes songs
> about summer. Who you reply to, when, and whether you leave them on read — every choice
> writes your ending.
>
> A story that never leaves the phone: messages, calls, group chats, Moments (friend feed),
> voicemail, tarot, puzzles and 17 endings — all rendered as apps on a phone you hold.

**Full description**:

- **A phone you live inside** — no dialogue boxes. The story arrives as texts at 11 PM,
  missed calls with consequences, and group chats with too many opinions.
- **17 endings** across 4 routes (3 love interests + a solo "true ending" route), including
  4 hidden ones that require collecting clues across runs.
- **New Game+** — carry your collection, achievements and ending gallery into a second run
  with exclusive dialogue variants and a post-true-ending epilogue.
- **Three-axis affection** — closeness / trust / tension each drive different scenes:
  encounters build intimacy, thoughtful gifts build trust, and high tension earns you
  midnight confessions.
- **Fully bilingual** — complete English and Chinese, switchable in Settings.
- **Playable offline** — installable PWA with full offline support.
- **Procedural everything** — every wallpaper, CG, avatar and piece of music is generated
> by code (Web Audio synthesis + generative art). No external assets, no tracking.
- ~2-3 hours per route. Free, no ads, no in-app purchases.

**Controls**: Mouse / touch. Keyboard playable (D1.1 accessibility pass).
